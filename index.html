A few things to know.

What is public?

The wallet-to-xx association in the registry and .skr ownership are public and historically observable. A .skr name is not an encryption key. Messages travel through cMix, not Solana. MMIS makes no absolute anonymity or zero-metadata claim. Local history is app-private plaintext; encrypted backups protect exported copies.

What does “Sent to network” mean?

The sender submitted the message to the network. It is not a Delivered or Read receipt. Sends can fail, time out or arrive late; an explicit retry can duplicate content. Incoming banners currently appear only while the app is in the foreground.

Mainnet registry & beta status

Registry protocol v1 · Upgradeable beta. Android only. Discovery depends on RPC availability; direct addresses bypass that lookup. Managing a listing requires explicit wallet approval and may incur Mainnet fees.
